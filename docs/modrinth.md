# Better Battle

**The Build Battle update Hypixel hasn't made.** Better Battle tracks every Build Battle game you play, grades your builds, gives you achievements and an Aspiring Level, and adds a proper timer and records to Speed Builders. Your profile, rank and records live at **[battle.aspir3.net](https://battle.aspir3.net)**.

## What it does

### Every game, saved
- Each game is saved on your computer first, then synced. Losing connection never loses a game.
- A short recap in chat after every game: placement, grade, crowd score, XP and anything you unlocked.
- **Last Game** in the menu shows the full breakdown, round by round.

### Speed Builders, finally taken seriously
- A stopwatch while you build, with your best time on that build next to it.
- Personal bests for every build, and Better Battle world records with a leaderboard for each build.
- Precision: how many of your clicks actually ended up in the build.

### 120+ achievements
- Many unlock straight away from your existing Hypixel stats.
- Challenges for every mode: win using only wood, win a 16-player Solo game, guess a build within 3 seconds…
- Speed Builders challenges: **Assembly Line** (one block type at a time), **Measure Twice** (no left clicks), **Photo Finish**, **Speedrunner** and more.
- Three **weekly challenges** every Monday, for XP toward your Aspiring Level.

### On the website
- A global ranking of every Build Battle player on Hypixel, not just mod users.
- Your profile: rank history, activity heatmap, achievements and how rare they are, Speed Builders bests.
- Compare yourself with a friend, and share a profile card in Discord.

## Play it your way

Press **B** and open **Settings**: the timer, the recap, pop-ups, chat messages and the rest can each be switched off. With everything off you're playing normal Build Battle, and your games are still saved.

## Fair play

Better Battle only **reads** chat, the scoreboard and your own clicks. It never presses anything for you, never shows where blocks go, and never helps you guess in Guess the Build. Whether a mod is allowed is up to Hypixel, so check their rules on allowed modifications; as with any mod, you use it at your own risk.

## Getting started

1. Install the jar for your version (Fabric 26.3 needs **Fabric API** too).
2. Join Hypixel and play Build Battle.
3. Press **B** for your level, achievements and weekly challenges.
4. To claim your website profile: sign up at battle.aspir3.net, open Account, get a code and type `/bb link <code>` in game.

**Commands:** `/bb` · `/bb achievements` · `/bb recap` · `/bb weekly` · `/bb news` · `/bb settings` · `/bb sync` · `/bb palette`

## Privacy

The mod sends your finished games and your achievements to the Better Battle server so your profile can show them. It never sees your Microsoft password: logging in goes through Mojang. Read the [privacy policy](https://battle.aspir3.net/privacy) for the full list, and delete your account or download your data at any time from the website.
